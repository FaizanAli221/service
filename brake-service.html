Brake Danger Signs You Shouldn't Ignore

High-Pitched Squealing

Wear indicator tab rubbing on rotor surface warning pad is under 3mm.

Steering / Pedal Vibration

Warped or unevenly worn brake rotors causing pulsation under braking.

Vehicle Pulling Left/Right

Seized caliper piston or restricted brake hose applying uneven friction.

Spongy / Soft Pedal

Air trapped in hydraulic lines or moisture degrading DOT fluid pressure.
