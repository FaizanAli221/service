When Should You Change Your Oil?

Mileage Threshold

Every 5,000 to 7,500 miles for modern synthetic lubricants.

Dark / Gritty Oil

Black, tar-like oil with metallic flecks indicates contamination.

Engine Ticking Noise

Low oil pressure or viscosity causes valvetrain and lifter noise.

Burning Oil Odor

Oil leaking onto hot exhaust manifolds or blow-by vapor.
